## Runtime profile (module 13)

Matching (SuperPoint+LightGlue) dominates at ~5.1–5.8 s of the ~5.2–5.8 s
total; ANMS 2–6 ms, MAGSAC++ <1 ms, ECC 15–19 ms. Runtime feasibility on CPU
is therefore a matcher-cost question, not a pipeline one — future work cites
ONNX/fp16 export of the matcher.

## KAGUYA cross-mission (the honest result)

- Both tiles ingest (12288², 7.4 m/px); the S42–45 tile is complete.
- The TMC-2 nca 2024 strip genuinely falls inside its footprint.
- Because the patch catalog carries ~±0.25° coordinate uncertainty, naive
  crops missed; the driver therefore ran **retrieval-driven localization**
  (1225 GSD-matched windows, LunaDNA top-5 → geometric verification).
- Both queries localized to the **same region** (sim 0.735/0.734, cross-mission
  embeddings are weaker than in-domain ~0.9), but geometric verification found
  ≤5 matches (< the 8-inlier guard). Cross-mission **localization** works;
  cross-mission **registration** is documented as the next milestone
  (per-window multi-scale matching, control-net co-registration).
